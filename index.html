Support

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Outlier: Party Game is a party game for friends in the same room: find the faker before they fool you. Everyone plays on their own iPhone.

Questions, bugs or ideas

Email outlierpartygames@gmail.com, or in the app open Settings > Report a problem. We read every message.

Common questions

What can we play? Outlier (one player doesn't get the secret word; with 5 or more players you can play with Two Outliers), Framed (draw or write your answer, but one player got a different prompt), Clocked (Spot On: stop the clock on the target time; Clocked Outlier: one player doesn't know the target) and Mixup (the game picks the mode each round).

How many players? Outlier, Framed and Clocked Outlier need 3 or more. Clocked Spot On works from 1 player. Up to 10 players per room.

Can I play alone or offline? Yes, Clocked Spot On. Host a room alone and pick Clocked. With no internet, tapping Host opens an offline room on your phone (it says OFFLINE where the room code would be).

How do friends join? The host shares the room code or link; everyone joins from their own phone.

Is there an account? No sign-in is needed. Your name is saved on your phone.

Are there ads? Yes. The app is free and shows a short full-screen ad from Google AdMob every 3 rounds, when the host starts the next round, and one when a game ends. Never in the middle of a round, and never in the offline room. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I remove ads? Buy Remove Ads (a one-time purchase) in Settings > Purchases. You never see ads, and nobody sees ads in rooms you host. On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases. Got a code from us? Tap Redeem and enter it in Apple's window.

Something went wrong in a room? If your connection drops, the app reconnects you and keeps your score. If the host leaves, the crown passes to another player.
