Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: there are no accounts. The game sends your player name and your moves to our room server so your friends can play with you, and deletes them when the room ends. Google shows ads and collects some device data to do so; personalised ads only if you allow it. Apple handles every payment. Your stats stay on your phone. We never sell your information.

Who we are

Outlier: Party Game ("Outlier", "we") is made by Michael McClellan, an independent developer. Contact: outlierpartygames@gmail.com.

Ads

Outlier is free and shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC): one every 3 rounds when the host starts the next round, and one when a game ends. Ads never play during a round, in the offline room, or in rooms whose host bought Remove Ads. We ask Google for ads rated no higher than Teen.

  • What Google collects. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your IP address (for approximate location), device and app information, identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), ad interactions, and diagnostics such as performance and crash data. Google may share this with its advertising partners, including other ad networks and ad technology providers that bid to show an ad.
  • Personalised ads use data about you from other apps and websites. You only get them if you tap Allow on Apple's tracking prompt and, where the law requires a consent form, agree in Google's form.
  • Non-personalised ads are what everyone else gets. They are based on context such as the app and your approximate location, and Google still uses data to count views, cap how often you see an ad and prevent fraud.
  • Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). The app asks once, on a Results screen, never at first launch. You can change your answer any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google's consent form. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, Google's consent message appears before any ad. It lets you accept, refuse or choose purposes and partners and, in those US states, opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for advertising. To answer again, delete and reinstall the app, and the form shows again before the next ad.
  • Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, How Google uses advertising data, Google's list of ad technology providers and My Ad Center (your Google ad settings).

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment: we never see your name, Apple ID, card or other payment details. Apple only tells the app whether this Apple ID owns Remove Ads, so it can turn ads off; the app remembers that on your phone. When you host, the room records that its host owns Remove Ads so no one in your room gets ads. Restore Purchases asks Apple again, and Redeem opens Apple's own offer-code window. Apple's handling of your data is in Apple's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms: when you leave a room, you are removed from it, and the last player out deletes the room. A room nobody closes is deleted automatically about 6 hours after its last activity (a cleanup runs every 15 minutes). Everything in a room (names, device IDs, votes, answers, scores) goes with it.
  • Framed drawings: removed when the round is over, and never kept longer than 6 hours.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own policies (links above).
  • On your phone: until you delete the app.

Your choices and rights

You can refuse tracking, refuse or change ad consent as described above, buy Remove Ads to stop ads, leave any room, or delete the app to erase everything stored on your phone. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. Email outlierpartygames@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Because there are no accounts, room data has usually been deleted by the time you write. For data Google collects for ads, use the Google links above.

Children

Outlier's App Store age rating is 9+. It is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we tell Google the app is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, email outlierpartygames@gmail.com.

Security

Data between the app and our providers travels encrypted (HTTPS).
