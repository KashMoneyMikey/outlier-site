Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Who we are

Outlier: Party Game ("Outlier") is made by an independent developer. Contact: outlierpartygames@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your player name. You type a name to play. It is shared with the other players in your room so they can see it, and saved on your phone for next time. Rooms are temporary and end when the last player leaves.
  • Game activity in a room. Votes, scores and game moves are sent between the phones in your room so the game can run. In Framed, your finished drawing is shared with the other players in that round (after the drawing time is over) so everyone can see every board, and it is removed when the round moves on to voting. None of this is kept after the room closes.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, an anonymous report (device model, OS version, app version and what went wrong) is sent to Sentry, our crash-report provider, so we can fix it. It does not include your name, contacts, location or messages.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. No advertising IDs, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no ads.

Where data lives

Room data is handled by Supabase, our backend provider. Crash reports are handled by Sentry (sentry.io), our crash-report provider, in the United States.

Deleting your data

Leaving a room removes you from it, and rooms are deleted when they end. Your saved name stays only on your phone; deleting the app removes it. You can also email outlierpartygames@gmail.com.

Children

Outlier is made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only thing you type is a nickname, which is shared only with the players in your room.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
